The sidebar

The sections of this page are in the sidebar, under the site's pages: the template below fills the sidebar block of base.html, which is how a page adds links of its own there. On a small screen the sidebar is a drawer the menu button opens, and the sections stay out of it, since a link within the page would leave it open.

A form validated on the server

With htmx the form posts into its own container and gets it back, with its errors or with the result and a message swapped in out of band; while the request runs the button is disabled and the indicator shows. Without JavaScript it posts to the same view and gets the whole page.
